# Personal Wealth Master Plan (PWMP) — host-driven governance protocol, v5.6

**When to load:** The user asks for a comprehensive wealth plan, ongoing personal financial management, financial independence strategy, or a review of an existing plan. For a narrow factual question, do not force a Master Plan. This is a **client-owned, revisable decision document**, NOT an Agent, brokerage allocator, managed account, trust document or automated service.

**The core question:** "Which money belongs to this person, which future obligations must it cover, which decisions should govern the rest, and how will we know whether the decisions worked?"

Follow `professional-consultation.md`, `value-and-behavior.md`, `high-income-crossborder-trader.md` when applicable, and the original twelve-state workflow in `skill-first-execution.md`. The Master Plan is a **deliverable**, not an 11th domain or 13th state.

## Minimal six-stage host workflow

1. **Map the client's reality (D01–D03 / INTAKE–HOUSEHOLD_SOLVENCY).** Establish as-of dates, country/state/city, tax residence **claims**, all accounts by economic owner, currency, bank deposits, personally owned brokerage **net equity**, restricted accounts, business trading capital, liabilities and debt terms. Record gross/realized/received/unrealized/PF simulated separately. Never add monthly profit to NAV again. Validate incoming/outgoing transfers so they are not counted as new wealth. Inventory insurance/pension rights as benefits, not fungible cash.
2. **Translate aspirations into obligations (D04 / GOAL_AND_LIFECYCLE).** Identify at least one goal, the intended date, amount **in target spending currency**, importance (essential or optional), real vs nominal denomination, unknowns, who depends on it, and what would constitute completion. Short-term commitments and multi-decade plans receive different funding and liquidity constraints. If there is no target amount/date, present conditional **goal-discovery policy**, not fictional financial independence numbers.
3. **Assign distinct economic purposes to assets (D03/D04/D07).** Borrow the *organizational idea* of purpose-based planning (Liquidity / Lifestyle / Growth / Legacy, or Liquidity / Longevity / Legacy), never claim it is a mathematically optimal proprietary formula. Build **one single owned-asset ledger**, then earmark *pieces of those assets* to named purposes. Typical purposes: `LIQUIDITY`, `LIFESTYLE`, `LONGEVITY`, `GROWTH`, `LEGACY`, `OPERATING`, `TAX_RESERVE`, `UNASSIGNED`. This is an **analytical allocation** only; no account is moved by labeling it. Reconcile: each asset's summed earmarks <= its owned eligible market value. Tax/operating/debt money should not automatically fund long-horizon risk assets. Different currencies may not be summed without dated FX.
4. **Write the client's Personal Wealth Policy (D08/D09).** Create 3–7 *decision rules*, NOT a fund catalog: (a) receive and reconcile; (b) protect essential lifestyle, contractual obligations and unverified tax liabilities; (c) determine trading/operating capital risk budget; (d) fund dated goals; (e) select only legally accessible long-horizon investment policy candidates **after eligibility, objectives and risk capacity verified**; (f) shock and review rules; (g) authority escalation. Each rule states `WHEN → THEN → BLOCK IF → WHO APPROVES → HOW TO CHECK`. Where tax status is unresolved, show conditional policy and professional handoff **without freezing unrelated non-tax decisions**.
5. **Compare competing policies (D05/D06/D08/D09).** Include status quo and one materially different alternative, and at least one downside or income-stop sensitivity suitable to the client's situation. Compare the same personally owned starting capital, accounting horizon, currencies, tax/fee assumptions, liquidity and life satisfaction. Separate additional retained principal vs hypothetical investment gain vs transfer/reallocation vs avoided interest/expenses. Prefer reversible human-led steps; preserve material theory conflicts instead of inventing consensus. No fixed return claim, no automatic trading and no exact unsupported tax estimate.
6. **Make it governable and revisable (D09/D10 / POLICY_CHECK–EXPLAIN_AND_MONITOR).** Handoff to the client with (i) a one-screen executive decision brief, (ii) asset/liability and goal registers, (iii) purpose earmarks and funding waterfall, (iv) policy charter, (v) 30/90/365-day action owners and status, (vi) review scorecard and material-change triggers, (vii) unresolved issues and human referrals. User chooses, authorizes and executes. An action is not `DONE` because a report recommends it. Persistent memory is opt-in and short-summary only; each L1 read/L2 write needs scope and approval. No L3 autonomous transfer, trading, opening accounts or tax filing.

## Required output and "missing-information" behavior

Use [../assets/personal-wealth-master-plan-template.md](../assets/personal-wealth-master-plan-template.md) for full plans. Every recommendation must answer **what purpose it serves, what it displaces, what conditions permit it, who can decide, and how to test success**. If asset values or tax liabilities are missing: mark net worth / free investable cash **UNKNOWN**, give an illustrative funding policy and priority-ranked information requests, but still deliver useful life-goal segmentation, conditional downside protection and action owners. Do not stop at "consult a tax adviser" or "diversify". For well-managed users, `MAINTAIN_AND_MONITOR` is a legitimate outcome.

**Mandatory checks:** 1) one personally owned asset counted only once; 2) non-owned PF simulated capital cannot be earmarked; 3) any exact money amount has currency and date; 4) reported/bank-confirmed/assumed are not interchangeable; 5) legally blocked actions remain blocked, but non-tax plan work continues; 6) no user actions are marked executed without proof; 7) every current policy specifies review triggers; 8) short-term goal cash not treated as long-horizon risk capital; 9) no 10–20y projection presented as realized gain; 10) the user can *revise or reject* the charter.

**Optional static integrity check:** Host may write its proposed structured plan to a private local JSON and run `python <SKILL_DIR>/scripts/master_plan_quality.py --input <PRIVATE_JSON>` (schema: `schemas/master-plan-v5.6.schema.json`). Program checks fields, ownership, earmark reconciliation and authorization-related contradictions only; it does not decide financial suitability or substitute for expert legal review. Do not put personal plans, account IDs or tax documentation into this Git repository.

## Review contract, not a phantom managed service

- **Monthly (on demand / scheduled only if host actually authorized):** received income, expense/drawdown, reserves, savings from true new money, current policy deviations, actions confirmed by user.
- **Quarterly or significant event:** goal funding coverage, upcoming liabilities, tax/legal changes, income concentration, portfolio drawdown and permitted strategic policy review.
- **Annually:** full net-worth reconciliation, user preferences, lifecycle schedule, real spending requirement, tax and fiduciary handoff status, lessons from previous decisions.
- Do not convert daily market headlines into frequent policy turnover, and do not promise follow-up without a scheduler.
