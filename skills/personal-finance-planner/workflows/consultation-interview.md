# High-information financial consultation interview — v5.3

## Objective

Find the **smallest set of additional facts that would materially change the decision**, not a maximal questionnaire. For all values capture date, currency, tax/gross/net/realized status, source and uncertainty. Do not ask again for facts already reliably available from the current conversation or authorized prior profile. Do not access saved private memory without L1 approval.

## Interview stages

| Stage | Decision it unlocks | Material facts | If not supplied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Scope and outcomes | What problem are we solving? | Up to three prioritized life/wealth goals, deadlines, target currency, definition of success | Offer illustrative pathways without claiming personalized plan |
| 2. Legal and income | What is *actually* free cash flow? | Current residence, potential tax residence(s), tax year, income type and stability, amount gross vs deposited, unresolved taxes | Net investable cash = **unknown** if tax or net income material and unresolved |
| 3. Balance sheet | What money belongs to this person? | Owned/unrestricted cash, restricted assets, liabilities and APR/terms; PF simulated funds excluded | Show partial statement + blockers |
| 4. Household resilience | Can the household absorb a shock? | Essential and discretionary monthly spend, dependants, insurance gaps, debt service, near-term obligations | Use ranges, request bills needed to confirm |
| 5. Feasibility | What levers can change the outcome? | Income growth options, savings flexibility, work/retirement horizon, time and risk constraints | Illustrate lever sensitivity with declared assumptions |
| 6. Implementation | Will the plan get done? | User preferences, automatic savings capability, time budget, review cadence, opt-in state policy | Suggest reversible manual next step |

## Question budget

For a full plan, ask up to **five highest-impact questions per interview round**, explain briefly what each answer changes, then proceed with verified data. Example initial round: (1) income already received net of withholding/tax? (2) essential/discretionary monthly spend? (3) owned accessible cash and debt APR? (4) top goal amount and deadline? (5) household/dependants and jurisdiction/tax residency? Combine already known facts instead of repeating.

Avoid asking for exact home addresses, tax ID/SSN, bank logins, account numbers, identity documents or private transaction exports. Refer users to their own confidential systems for records. Use user-confirmed aggregates first, then read-only authorized sources where available.

## Information-value priority

Question priority = whether its answer can change a hard constraint (legal, solvency) > alter the feasible option set (amount, timing, debt rate, eligibility) > change ranking between feasible choices > only improve narrative detail. Do not attach invented probabilistic precision to these priorities.

If data are contradictory, present BOTH statements and ask which date/source supersedes; retain a clear `UNKNOWN` until resolved. A saved fact is context, **not** a current verification.
