# Mathematical models and report limits

- Legacy v0.4: deterministic multicurrency-per-bucket cash flow snapshot and legal research tasks.
- v5: fixed-return annuity goal algebra; user-assumed portfolio fee/variance; optional hypothetical progressive marginal brackets; seeded Monte Carlo with monthly normal returns; FI initial withdrawal screening. **None are live calibrated return/tax estimates.**
- Real-vs-nominal: `(1+nominal annual return)/(1+annual inflation)-1` when consistent. Real target goals are converted to nominal target at goal date.
- Monte Carlo reporting: user-supplied growth, inflation, volatility, seed, paths and explicit goal basis are mandatory. State model limitation, path count, scenario sensitivity and no guarantee. Do not claim sampled success is true probability.
- Source citations should distinguish verified current research from original archived reference pointers. If the skill has no actual live host source, state 'not retrieved'.
- Output: owner cash flow, obligations, separate currencies, reserve funding, taxes unverified, evidence and tensions, risk bounds, target feasibility, alternatives, source/review gates. Explain why uncertain conclusions are blocked.
