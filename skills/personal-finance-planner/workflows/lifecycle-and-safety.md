# Household solvency and lifecycle

Plan order: verified owned net receipts → contractual debt payments → essential living expenses → tax/insurance reserves → short-horizon spending goals → emergency buffer → longer-horizon capital.

- Distinguish bank cash, tradable securities, restricted retirement, housing, insurance, public pensions, joint/unknown beneficial ownership and PF simulated capital.
- Reserve-month heuristic is parameterized by income stability; it is not a universal optimal level. Actual cash (not stock ETFs) is needed to meet emergency bills, consider liquidity/regional deposit safety.
- Flag APR, rate reset, missed debt payments, medical coverage, disability, dependants, child/education, housing goals, succession and asset domicile. Do not recommend a policy or product without licensed/eligibility review.
- Keep near-term liabilities in own currency. Securities may be short-term liquid technically but not reliable emergency funding after a market crash.
- Scenario math can assess ability to save, not whether a person has adequate insurance, estate plans or social security entitlements.
