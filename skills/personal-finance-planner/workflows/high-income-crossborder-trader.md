# High-income / multi-account / cross-border trader consultation — Skill-First v5.5

**Trigger:** A client reports material PF payouts, personal brokerage P&L, cross-border bank receipts, business income, securities margin, or exceptionally variable trading-linked cash flows. This is a specialization of the original **D01/D02/D03/D04/D08/D09/D10** domains, **not** an eleventh domain or another Agent. Read with `professional-consultation.md`, `value-and-behavior.md`, and `wealth-ownership-ledger.md`. The HOST conducts interview, primary-source research, calculations and recommendations. The Skill does not read accounts, execute trades, or initiate transfers.

## Stage A — establish what the user ACTUALLY owns (avoid both overcounting and overexcluding)

Make a dated three-ledger reconciliation **before** calling anything “monthly income” or “net assets”:

1. **Bank cash flow ledger:** separate PF challenge/simulated profits, platform payments *eligible* for withdrawal, payout requested/approved, payout actually credited (user claimed vs documented), company revenue vs personally owned receipt, business fees and potential repayment/clawback. Hong Kong bank location is a payment rail, not proof of HK tax residence or tax exemption. If the user says “收入在香港汇丰卡”, classify as **USER_REPORTED claimed receipt**, not independently VERIFIED. Do not pretend to have seen statements unless the host actually read them with scoped L1 permission.
2. **Own brokerage account ledger:** record **net account equity** at a dated snapshot (net of margin borrowing and brokerage liabilities), tradable cash, restricted/margin-encumbered assets, realized trading P&L, unrealized P&L, realized proceeds, dividends, and **actual withdrawals to bank**. Personally owned securities can belong to *net worth* even before selling, but valuation and access constraints apply. **Do not** add reported monthly profit to brokerage NAV or to cash if already embedded in NAV. Withdrawal broker→bank **increases spendable bank cash**, but is an **internal transfer**, not new household wealth; include fees/taxes as separate costs. Do not assume monthly profits are recurring forever.
3. **Debt and ownership ledger:** record vehicle-loan outstanding principal, APR/effective rate, remaining term, early-payment fee, monthly debt service interest/principal split, and any other claims. Debt service is a cash obligation; *principal repayment lowers debt* and is not entirely economic consumption. Identify whether holdings are personal, joint, company-owned, or in simulation; avoid totals without ownership proof. Equity losses can make cash and portfolio deteriorate simultaneously.

**Critical requirement:** For users with both PF payouts and own trading account, produce **both** a monthly bank cash-flow picture and a dated personally owned **balance sheet**. If brokerage NAV and liabilities are missing, label net worth UNKNOWN; do not simply discard owned investments or assert the user is already wealthy from monthly P&L.

## Stage B — reconcile inherited expense context; do not discard or assume it

If the user says “the rest of my spending is as previously described,” merge reliable prior-chat facts and explicitly mark *unreconfirmed* items. For example, if the current message names rent, basic living and car-loan payments while earlier conversation named leisure, calculate both a **currently reported obligation baseline** and a **fuller-lifestyle variant** including the *unreconfirmed* leisure item. Neither is magically VERIFIED. Show reserve targets by multiplying each relevant monthly cash-need scenario by the chosen reserve months. Treat twelve months as a **modifiable heuristic**, not optimum.

Do not infer that one good month of cash receipts means the expense reserve has been **funded**. Current unrestricted bank cash, earmarked taxes, trading operating needs, near-term debts and commitments must be known before asserting a reserve gap or how much can go to investment.

## Stage C — tax and cross-border policy: research actively but keep independent planning open

- Map **residence vs claimed tax residence(s), time spent, citizenship/domicile where material, activity location, payer/counterparty, personal vs entity ownership, income nature, custody bank and investment assets** for relevant dates and tax year. Mainland China, Hong Kong and any market/payer jurisdictions may have distinct roles. Research operative official law using actually available host browser/search and date the source. Consider individual reporting, source and character of income, tax treaty/tax-credit questions, cross-border remittance and FX constraints, beneficial ownership and estate implications **only when material**. Do not infer legal tax residence from a card location or quote an unverified tax rate as fact.
- An unexplained 35%–45% reserve **is not a legally determined tax rate**. You may show a *user-labeled sensitivity scenario*, explaining it is neither estimate nor safe recommended provision. Never choose a definitive tax percentage on generic intuition.
- **Do not STOP all financial planning** because exact tax is unknown. Separate **blocked legally actionable decisions** (precise tax payable, specific tax-optimized trades, permitted account access) from **available conditional decisions** (cash-flow ranges, preserve documents, reserve policy structure, review investment concentration, debt-term check, lifestyle sustainability, alternate financing paths). A referral alone is not a financial plan.

## Stage D — construct the client's wealth architecture and cash-waterfall

For the monthly *reported settled personal external cash*, show a policy waterfall with **explicit unknowns**, not fixed universal percentages:

`owned bank credits (documented or provisional) → recognize legally due taxes, real operating costs, contracted necessities and debt → protect emergency and committed near-term cash needs → preserve separately necessary trading operating capital → consider long-horizon personally owned asset funding only with after-tax available resources and the client's goal/risk/legal constraints.`

This is a **decision dependency order, not an executable instruction to move funds automatically**. If personal brokerage assets already hold part of the long-term wealth, distinguish redistribution across risky assets from *new retained earnings*. Name existing accounts as control domains only, not literal mandatory new bank accounts.

For each material lever provide: `CURRENT FACT → ACCOUNTING/RISK MECHANISM → ALTERNATIVES (including wait/no-change) → CONDITIONAL MATHEMATICAL DIFFERENCE → COST + DOWNSIDE → USER ACTION → CHECK AT NEXT REVIEW`.

Required options in a major high-income full plan (unless inapplicable):
- **Defensive:** segregate documented obligations and adequate liquid buffer; stop unverified large commitments; verify tax and owned balances.
- **Balanced policy:** after verification, document a target split between trading operations, short goals and long-horizon owned assets; use planned releases tied to actual *after-tax available* cash, not paper P&L.
- **Business-continuity alternative:** retain more capital in the user's trading operation, but price lost diversification, counterparty risk and drawdown exposure; do not assume its future return is repeatable.

**No recommended allocation percentage** unless sufficient inputs support it. A conditional ranking with clear dependency gates is still valuable. Do not frame large monthly brokerage P&L as a sustainable wage without multi-period verified records.

## Stage E — model meaningful simultaneous stress, not single-variable happy-path

At minimum compare (i) normal *verified* receipts if available, (ii) months with PF payouts stopping, (iii) brokerage losses *while* receipts stop, (iv) unexpected tax payment/required reporting and loan debt service; (v) inability to withdraw capital during margin/counterparty stress when material. Run 6/12-month **expense cash need** estimates, but if liquid resources are UNKNOWN, runway and survivability are **UNKNOWN**. Do not invent the distribution or confidence of extreme returns.

Goal modeling is optional until a **real age, current balance sheet, target and deadlines** are provided. Avoid fake 20-year FI estimates based only on one high income month. Show which information could unlock the next calculation.

## Stage F — client-facing delivery (do not hide value behind 8 sections of uncertainty)

Deliver the following **even with incomplete data**, in approximately this order:

1. **Executive decision board:** one sentence on what is and is not known; top three decisions *the client can make now* and the most material unknown. Use personalized amounts, never claim wealth size without NAV.
2. **Two-ledger reconciliation table:** (a) personal bank inflows/expenses/surplus **before unresolved tax+business costs**, (b) owned financial assets/broker NAV & debts — UNKNOWN explicitly. Show separate statuses for a PF payout and a brokerage-performance figure.
3. **Wealth policy / cash waterfall:** size known core reserve, show optional lifestyle variant, conditional funding logic with blocked or deferred capital movements, and review impact of a loan early payoff **only after APR/principal/penalties known**.
4. **At least two materially distinct strategy options**, uncertainty, trade-off and implementation gates; no guess at investment product selection.
5. **30 / 90 / 365-day actions** with owner, exact next document/decision, objective metric, and current status PROPOSED/CONFIRMED/OBSERVED. Do not assign DONE on implication.
6. **Top five high-information questions** only; ask for aggregated data, not exact account IDs / secrets. High-value candidates: (a) bank actual PF credits vs platform P&L + operating costs, (b) broker net equity/margin/withdrawals/realization of $100k–$300k, (c) current unrestricted bank cash + loan APR/principal, (d) financial goals/age/household and whether prior discretionary spending remains, (e) material tax residence/current returns/payer/entity structure. Avoid repeating known data.
7. **Value and follow-up contract:** for each proposed action say what user behavior should change and **how we will measure effect next month** (e.g., reconciled bank receipts, tax reserve coverage, trading-to-longterm *net new* retained amount, cash runway, loan effective rate, portfolio market concentration). Predicted vs realized vs caused-by-Skill must stay separate.

### Host version and privacy integrity checks

Read the **installed** SKILL.md `metadata.version` and report it. The user's observed report may say `5.1.0` while GitHub has a newer version: warn that updating GitHub alone does NOT update installed Skill. If host can inspect installed Skill path, include it without private home path leakage in public reports. Do not claim v5.5 ran unless that version was actually loaded. Prior-plan comparison requires L1 authorized memory access; saves need separately approved L2.

Never promise files placed in `/tmp` are automatically deleted on session restart. Use OS-safe protected temporary storage when unavoidable, minimize personal content, and explicitly clean up, but understand backups and logs may persist. Prefer **stdin** for transient optional scripts and do not echo raw personal data to terminal transcripts or GitHub.

Optional single-currency **arithmetic-only** helper: `scripts/trader_wealth_reconcile.py --input -` reads JSON from stdin; synthetic example is `examples/v5.5-crossborder-mixed-trader-SYNTHETIC.json`. If in doubt, do the accounting directly using authorized host math tools. Its output is NOT an official tax, bank balance, legal, or portfolio recommendation.
