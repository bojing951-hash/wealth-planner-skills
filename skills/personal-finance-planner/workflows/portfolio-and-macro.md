# Portfolio and macro research (10–30 year horizon)

- Begin with goals, tax residence, lawful access, cash buckets, market-risk capacity and risk of loss. **Do not start from a ticker.**
- Use long-run evidence/expected return ranges to build an explicit, user-approved assumption bundle. Separate observed local-currency real yield from forward equity risk premium, inflation expectations, foreign exchange and tax drag. Risk premia are not guaranteed.
- Use Markowitz covariance for conditional diversification, Sharpe's cost identity for after-fee comparison, Bogle as low-maintenance candidate baseline, Graham as conditional price/value framework, Campbell-Shiller for imperfect long-run valuation information, ALM and tail-risk stress for short-horizon protections. Do not average contradictory policy styles into a pseudo-optimal number.
- Never allocate all user assets to risk funds. Explicitly separate true cash safety reserves, near-term goals, tax reserves and speculative/active accounts.
- Use `portfolio` command with **user/host-labelled expected returns and covariance**, validate PSD. Not an optimizer. Fees shown omit personal tax, spread, FX and product domicile. Review legal investment access and pension wrappers.
- Simulations are reproducible hypothetical scenarios with seeded stochastic normal draws, not estimated historical probabilities; stress changes inputs or shocks explicitly. Review shocks correlated with income when realistic.
- Rebalance based on targets, cost, tax, market drift and goal changes, not because the last CPI surprised or a pundit forecast changed.
