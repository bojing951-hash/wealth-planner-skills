# Jurisdiction-aware legal and tax workflow

**Checklist, not automatic legal advice.** Determine where the individual actually lives and has tax residence; for US investigate federal citizenship/residency plus state and local; for China investigate domicile/183-day tests and current IIT rules; HK territorial source is activity-specific and does NOT annul obligations elsewhere; Thailand income/remittance depends on tax year; EU is not a single personal tax code.

1. Resolve actual year, employment/independent work/PF payout/capital gain/rental income characterization; payer vs bank vs activity is not necessarily income source.
2. Identify all relevant scopes: home, tax residence, citizenship, activity, payer, bank, asset situs.
3. Retrieve primary statute/official guides effective for tax year. Do NOT reuse rate from hypothetical test city comparisons as current law.
4. For each provision classify: applicability fact **verified**, **user-claimed**, **disputed**, **unknown**; stale or unmatched ⇒ block final tax calculations.
5. If legally actionable tax computation is beyond implemented verified rule sets, show only budgeted reserves or `HYPOTHETICAL_USER_SUPPLIED` sample-bracket calculation. The latter is not a local tax computation.
6. Identify pension/health care, inheritance, treaty, disclosures and FX restrictions where they affect investment pathways.
7. High impact cross-border structures / residency planning / citizenship tax ⇒ qualified local tax lawyer/accountant; no evasion or concealment advice.

`law_packets` when supplied are **date/domain/scope checks only**, not authoritative conclusions. Government domains may publish superseded PDFs and guidance. No country in v5 has a certified end-to-end tax engine.
