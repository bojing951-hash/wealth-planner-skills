# Personal Wealth Master Plan — client-facing template (v5.6)

> A living, client-controlled decision charter. Use the actual date, the relevant country/tax year, and *verified / user-reported / hypothetical / unknown* labels for material facts. If data are missing, provide a **CONDITIONAL** plan. Do not pretend this template is itself a completed financial plan.

## 4. Personal Wealth Policy (D08/D09)

For each important policy:

> **Policy ID & objective:** ...  
> **WHEN:** observable, dated trigger (e.g. credited payout / near-term expense / income interruption).  
> **THEN:** proposed rule or decision for user approval; cash waterfall or exposure limit.  
> **BLOCK IF:** unresolved legal, tax, liquidity or ownership conditions.  
> **WHO DECIDES / SPECIALIST:** user, and if needed an identified qualified professional.  
> **TRADE-OFF:** foregone liquidity, reduced business capital, time or wellbeing cost.  
> **HOW TO VERIFY:** specific record/metric and review date.  
> **STATUS:** PROPOSED / USER_APPROVED / DOCUMENTED_EXECUTED / BLOCKED / DEFERRED.

Policies cover actual income reconciliation, protected money and future goals before discussing asset products. If an investment policy is proposed, document **asset class, risk horizon, costs, legal access and rebalance guardrails**, not a fabricated optimal fund or guaranteed return.

## 7. Governance and ongoing reviews (D10)

**Monthly scorecard:** verified liquid reserves, realized new savings (exclude transfers), obligations, policy deviations, action proof.  
**Quarterly review:** funding gaps, upcoming liabilities, joint income/portfolio shocks, legal applicability, strategy stability.  
**Annual review:** complete asset-liability reconciliation, goals, tax/legal handoffs, financial independence purchasing power.  
**Event-triggered review:** relocation, marriage/dependants, major income loss, new debt, law change, material goal change.  
**Evidence and assumption log:** date, source/URL, rule effective date, model version, exact assumptions, unresolved contradictions.  
**Memory:** do not create/read/write local memory by default; if user specifically opts in, store a *short* dated policy/decision summary only, with separate approvals.

**User-friendly final test:** The client should be able to answer (1) what do I own? (2) what must it pay for? (3) what decision rule applies next? (4) who acts? (5) how do I know it worked? If any answer is missing, the master plan is incomplete.
