# Valuation and attribution register — Wealth Planner Skill v5.4

**Purpose:** Distinguish the financial value of a choice from investment return, causal impact, and lifestyle value. The host chooses methods; the optional calculator only checks algebra.

| Value claim | Sound way to estimate | Not valid without further evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Increased savings | Difference in owned **net** resources *actually retained*, after genuine consumption, outflows and any implementation costs, for matched time period | Treating a cash-to-equity transfer as a new deposit or net-worth gain |
| Compounding benefit | Identical rates/timing/currency for status-quo and proposal; compute capital contribution delta separately from hypothetical growth delta | Calling model growth **realized** income or treating market outperformance as a Skill effect |
| Lower management fees | Comparable asset exposure and invested principal, total all-in fees and turnover, tax consequences, fee accrual over time | Assuming two different portfolios earn identical risk-adjusted returns without disclosure, then calling fee delta risk-free |
| Lower debt interest | Full amortization or verified payoff terms; include penalties, liquidity/opportunity cost, refinancing fees, tax effects | Counting principal reduction as consumption or claiming the APR is a guaranteed investment return |
| Tax efficiency | User's actual jurisdiction/tax residency/income characterization, authoritative effective law and all compliance costs | Inventing tax rates, assuming an offshore bank account removes residence taxes |
| Reduced tail-risk | Reserve runway, shortfall and scenario survival, audited exposure changes | Inventing an avoided catastrophe or precise probability from uncalibrated scenarios |
| Saved time / wellbeing | Explicit user feedback, hours actually saved, degree of stress, expense satisfaction and family priorities | Pricing the user's leisure at assumed hourly wages without permission |
| Better goals | Time-matched real purchasing power and owned funds earmarked for actual life obligations | Pretending retirement assets are freely spendable today or double-counting pension/insurance values |

## Evidence maturity and attribution vocabulary

- **HYPOTHETICAL:** scenario if a behavior is sustained; not measured outcome.
- **USER_REPORTED_OBSERVATION:** dated user report; plausible but not independently verified.
- **DOCUMENTED_OBSERVATION:** host with explicit read approval has reviewed period-matched evidence; not causal inference.
- **ATTRIBUTED_CAUSAL_EFFECT:** only if the design genuinely supports attribution (credible comparator, confounding controls and assumptions). The v5.4 tools do **not** emit this state.

Explicitly separate *forecast* (what may happen) from *implemented* (action confirmed), *observed* (data changed), and *caused by advice* (requires stronger evidence). Preserve prior forecasts and mismatches; never silently rewrite baseline to appear successful.

## Primary standards for executing this protocol

CFP planning process, household budget accounting, lifecycle/asset-liability matching, and behavior-change research are **methods**, not permission to assert guaranteed results. For material regulatory, tax or market claims, retrieve and verify current primary sources with actual host tools; do not rely on this archived note as an up-to-date rule. User preference for wellbeing and low maintenance can outweigh numerical wealth maximization.
