# Personal wealth governance principles (PWMP v5.6)

## What professional planning adds beyond an ordinary AI answer

**A plan is a decision policy, not an allocation pie-chart.** Success includes preserved liquidity, feasible future liabilities, optionality, actually lower costs, adherence, fewer avoidable forced sales, and household wellbeing. A high-income client need not maximize risky assets; a low-income client need not adopt arbitrary saving percentages.

## Mandatory conceptual distinctions

- **Legal owner vs economic beneficiary vs accessible capital:** PF nominal challenge balances and leverage are not personally owned wealth. Personally beneficially owned *net* brokerage equity is wealth even if gains are unrealized; those gains are not automatically distributable free cash. Loan liability offsets owned assets on a consistent valuation basis. Do not add brokerage NAV and its realized/unrealized P&L separately.
- **Asset vs purpose:** `Liquidity`, `Lifestyle`, `Longevity`, `Growth`, `Legacy`, `Operating`, `Tax reserve` describe the **use of the same stock of resources**. They are not additional accounts in the balance sheet. One $100k deposit cannot fund both $100k emergency liquidity and $100k future goal simultaneously.
- **Net worth vs cashflow:** principal transferred from a checking account to a broker changes *location*, not household net worth. Monthly earnings can increase both wealth and cash only to the extent not offset by tax/debts/expenses; reconcile transactions.
- **Funding need vs investment idea:** goal amount/date/currency and probability-of-shortfall concern come **before** products; risk assets belong only to portions of wealth whose required withdrawal horizon permits the downside.
- **User preferences vs hard constraints:** human-defined minimum lifestyle and risk limits matter; do not invent preferred consumption cuts. Law, proof, liquidity and eligibility cannot be traded against hypothetical investment yield.
- **Income and portfolio are not independent by default:** an active trader with brokerage risk may lose earned income while portfolio assets fall. Include paired downside scenarios where relevant. Model strength of co-movement only with calibrated evidence; otherwise illustrate a range.

## Decision ownership and governance

| Decision / operation | Responsible party | Host role | Skill boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Define goals, consumption, risk willingness | User / family | Facilitate trade-offs | No imposed goal |
| Validate tax residence, income category, filing | Qualified local practitioner when material | Organize factual issues and verified law | No unsupported binding opinion |
| Verify owned balances and liabilities | User / authorized read-only source | Reconcile dated values | No implicit bank access |
| Approve long-term allocation *policy* | User with qualified advice if necessary | Show options and risk | No auto orders |
| Execute bank transfers / investments | User or authorized regulated service | No automatic L3 actions | Prohibited autonomous execution |
| Save/alter private history | User authorizes exact scope/change | Preview then optional local adapter | Never silently persist |
| Periodic assessment | User / authorized real scheduler | Check goal, cash, deviations | No fake background monitoring |

## Funding waterfall — order of questions, not a fixed universal budget

1. What net funds are **actually paid and personally owned**? For PF count bank payout only and reconcile costs; separate brokerage net equity, cash realized, transfer records.
2. Which contractual bills, debt obligations, business operating costs, and reasonably possible tax liabilities must be considered? Unknown tax => **not zero**; amount cannot be asserted without verified law.
3. What must be liquid to cover essential lifestyle, short-term goals, dependants and known obligations? Use user-specific reserve design; "6/12 months" are editable heuristics.
4. What capital must remain in an active business/trading model? Guard against reinvesting every trading payout due to recency bias. Allocation is conditional on operational sustainability.
5. Which funding tranches can truly support longer-dated goals? Show current eligibility, gaps, uncertainty and alternative horizons.
6. Only then compare long-term risk/cost/tax-efficient **policy** options; product selection can be deferred.

## Policy rule grammar

```text
RULE P-01 (example only)
WHEN: A new payout is documented and the bank balance is reconciled.
THEN: Prepare a proposed funds-availability statement after known bills and provisional tax/operating holds.
BLOCK IF: Tax category, ownership or short-term liquidity cannot be reconciled for an exact investable-amount claim.
OWNER/APPROVER: user; qualified tax professional for unresolved tax status.
EVIDENCE: dated bank receipt, known obligations, applicable law as actually retrieved.
METRIC: verified funds available for decision; actual transfer = none until separately approved.
REVIEW: each material payout or tax classification change.
```

**Not a promise to execute.** `PROPOSED`, `USER_APPROVED`, `DOCUMENTED_EXECUTED`, `BLOCKED`, `DEFERRED` must never be conflated. Evidence grades and source as-of date should be attached to a rule and an observation, not just a report-wide disclaimer.

## Lightweight policy scorecard

- `owned_net_worth_by_currency` with dated valuation and source quality; no forced FX consolidation.
- `current_liquid_coverage_months` derived from *accessible liquid funds* and verified essential spend (if either missing => UNKNOWN).
- `next_12m_liability_coverage`, using purpose earmarks only once.
- `reconciled_net_new_savings_by_period`, excluding internal transfers and unverified simulation profits.
- `tax_and_legal_open_items`, with owner/deadline and blocked actions.
- `goals_funded_vs_target` only for compatible real/nominal denomination and dates.
- `portfolio_income_joint_stress`, with explicit loss assumptions and no uncalibrated probability.
- `policy_exception_count` and `action_completion_proofs` with status not wishful thinking.
- `user_time_and_wellbeing_change` when the user wants to measure them.

When a recommendation has not been implemented, report **modeled effect** only. When a behavior changes, report **observed** result only if period-aligned credible evidence exists. Causality needs a defensible counterfactual and controls; it cannot be inferred just because a skill was loaded.
