# Decision rules / 个人财富规划的条件决策框架

## A. Inputs: convert all earnings into actual cash receipts

1. If payment has not reached an owned bank account and is reversible / hypothetical, don't count it as owned personal capital.
2. If tax status unclear, note the uncertainty; do not assume a zero-tax jurisdiction based solely on the receiving bank.
3. For volatile income, use a distribution from at least 12 months of actual receipts where available; otherwise explicitly disclose that the forecast lacks evidence.
4. Keep cash receipts, profit, investment market value, and account notional balance separate.

## B. Risk and liquidity policy

1. Normal cash flow = actual monthly cash receipts − essential spending − discretionary spending − obligatory operating expenses − due debt service − tax reserve.
2. Basic commitment basis = essential spending + unavoidable business costs + debt service; default months stable 6, variable/uncertain 12; user can change.
3. If net cash flow negative, prioritize household safety and durable income fixes before increasing financial risk.
4. If high-cost debt >= illustrative 8%, the simulator first retains a starter emergency buffer and assumes possible accelerated repayment. Confirm actual terms before proposing real-life execution.
5. Never take liquidity needed within the next 1–3 years and blindly allocate it to long-term volatile risk assets.
6. Do not double count personal financial assets and PF firm's funded notional trading capital.

## C. Investing policy (educational only)

- Segment emergency cash, near-term goal reserves, and long-term capital.
- Consider diversified domestic equity, regulated overseas exposure where eligible, bonds, gold, and cash, focusing on role, total costs, correlation, and liquidity.
- Avoid assuming the combination of several overlapping index products constitutes diversification.
- Track expense ratios, tracking error, trading spreads, FX effects, availability, taxes and investment duration.
- Position sizing must reflect the **whole economic balance sheet**, including employment/entrepreneurial risk and PF revenue dependence.

## D. Forecast and goal check

- Always show starting assets, annual return assumptions, horizon, and recurring cash flows.
- The time-axis is monthly and scenarios are deterministic. Do not call 5% the expected market return or describe scenario outputs as probabilities.
- Include a six-month income-interruption stress test, with elective consumption cut to zero by default.
- "Additional monthly cash required" is extra **net** receipts after any taxes and fees; cannot be directly equated to a gross salary increase.
- Present debt principal as a liability; monthly debt payments do not automatically increase investment assets. Avoid counting debt payments twice.
- Recompute after each income/spending/asset/debt update; monitor each result's input version and assumptions.

## E. Stop/hand-off conditions

- Current tax classification, residence and cross-border proceeds unclear: require specialist tax review rather than assuming rules.
- Specific security recommendation or public commercial service: review applicable licensing and suitability obligations.
- User tries to send credentials, full bank account numbers or personal IDs: do not request or store them in source control.
- Portfolio probability calculation requested without sourced historical series or distribution assumptions: provide deterministic examples and list what's needed for Monte Carlo rather than fabricate a probability.
