# Trading/household economic ownership — anti-double-counting reference (v5.5)

A financial plan needs **stock** (dated asset/liability balances), **flow** (money over time), and **valuation/P&L** (changes in owned assets) to be reconciled. These are not interchangeable. The following table describes financial accounting logic only; classification for tax purposes must be researched independently.

| Item | Bank cash flow | Personally owned wealth | Correct treatment / caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| PF evaluation account not owned by user | No | No | Not personal $50k/$100k capital. Simulation performance is not owned cash. |
| PF eligible payout not yet paid | Not yet | Depends on enforceable claim | Do not treat requested payout as bank income; review contract and conditions. |
| PF cash payout reported as credited to personal bank | USER_REPORTED receipt unless bank verified | Bank cash if actually owned | Clarify gross/net of fees, tax obligations and entity/beneficial ownership. |
| Own brokerage net equity (NAV after margin borrowings) | Not necessarily withdrawable | Financial asset, subject to volatility/restrictions | Snapshot, currency, account beneficial owner, margin treatment and last valuation. Don't classify all unrealized NAV as liquid cash. |
| Realized or unrealized trading gain | Does not imply bank receipt | Already embedded in owned brokerage NAV | Separate realized/unrealized performance for tax and risk; do not add again to NAV. |
| Withdrawal from brokerage into user's own bank | Bank +, brokerage - | Transfer (net wealth unchanged before fees/tax) | Useful for spendable liquidity, not newly earned household wealth. |
| Exchange USD bank balance to CNY bank balance | Internal transfer + FX | Same wealth at dated FX less fees/FX gains | Needs rate/date, country permissions; no invented tax exemption. |
| Loan installment | Bank cash - | Loan balance decreases by PRINCIPAL component | Interest/fees reduce net worth; principal payment reduces cash AND liability. Need amortization terms. |
| Personal company payment vs company balance | Depends on payout | Separate beneficial ownership | Company capital is not automatically personally unrestricted funds. |
| Household capital invested in broker ETF | Bank cash -, owned investments + | Transfer to market-risk asset; no new net principal | Future modeled difference due return and risk, not immediate $X created. |

**Checks before reporting financial independence:** actual owned unrestricted bank cash; net owned brokerage NAV with leverage encumbrances; business equity vs personal income; short debt/tax obligations; retirement/restricted funds; tax residence and income character; household commitments. An extraordinary monthly trading-profit number is not an annuity and does not prove a 20-year cash flow stream.

**Stress structure:** self-trading cash stops **AND** own broker investments lose value or become nonwithdrawable; expenses continue and tax liabilities may crystallize. Report runway only if adequate liquid balances and earmarked obligations are known. Show uncertainty without inventing precise extreme-event odds.
